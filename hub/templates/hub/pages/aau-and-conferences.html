    What this page is, and what it isn't

    A map of the two worlds a DMV player is seen in: the spring and summer shoe circuits (the top level of AAU, or "grassroots", basketball) and the high school conferences and leagues during the winter season. Every fact links to the circuit's or league's own site.

    No 2027 circuit dates yet

    As of 13 October 2026, none of the three shoe circuits had published its 2027 schedule on its official site. The dates below are last season's (2026), shown only so you know the usual shape of the season. Don't plan travel from them. Check each official site from early in the new year.1,3,4

    Spring and summer

    The three shoe circuits

    Each circuit is a league of club teams sponsored by one shoe company. Teams play a few weekend "sessions" from April to July, ending in a championship event. Players get there through a club team that's part of the circuit, not by signing up individually; the events and camps guide explains how that actually works.

    Nike EYBL

    Nike's circuit, ending at Peach Jam in North Augusta, South Carolina. Peach Jam has 17-Under, 16-Under and 15-Under divisions.1,2

    • 2026 for reference: sessions in Atlanta (24–26 April), Memphis (15–17 May), Kansas City (22–25 May) and Las Vegas (8–12 July); Peach Jam 14–19 July.1,2
    • Official site: nikeeyb.com

    adidas 3SSB (3 Stripes Select Basketball)

    adidas Basketball's grassroots program for boys and girls aged 12 to 18, with camps and tournaments through the year.3 Its home page shows no dated schedule; event dates are on the site's event pages.

    • Official site: adidas3ssb.com

    Under Armour Association (UAA)

    Under Armour's league-style circuit at 17U, 16U and 15U. Teams play 12 regular-season games, then a single-elimination bracket at the UAA Finals in July.4

    • 2026 for reference: Kansas City (17–19 April), Ohio (15–17 May), Georgia (9–12 July); Finals in Kentucky (16–19 July).4
    • Official site: underarmournext.com/basketball/boys-uaa

    Guidance — not a rule

    A circuit event only helps recruiting if college coaches are allowed to be there that weekend. The NCAA calendar decides that, and an event must be NCAA-certified for coaches to evaluate at it. Both are explained in When coaches can watch.

    Winter

    DMV high school conferences and leagues

    Where a school plays decides its schedule, its playoffs, and the transfer rules that apply. The transfer and NIL guide covers those rules in detail.

    Maryland

    • Public schools — MPSSAA. The state association for Maryland's public high schools.5 County systems such as Montgomery County run their own athletics programs within it.6
    • Private schools (boys) — MIAA. The Maryland Interscholastic Athletic Association.7
    • Private schools, girls — IAAM. The girls' private-school association.8

    The WCAC (Maryland, DC and Northern Virginia)

    The Washington Catholic Athletic Conference, formed in 1993: 13 private schools.9

    • Co-ed: Archbishop Carroll, Bishop Ireton, Bishop McNamara, Bishop O'Connell, Our Lady of Good Counsel, Paul VI Catholic, St. John's College, St. Mary's Ryken.
    • All-boys: DeMatha Catholic, Gonzaga College, The Heights School.
    • All-girls: Academy of the Holy Cross, Elizabeth Seton.

    The IAC — not yet verified

    The Interstate Athletic Conference is a private-school league in the DC area. Its official site blocked our check on 13 October 2026, so its member list isn't given here. Confirm membership with the school directly.

    Washington, DC

    • Public schools — DCIAA. The DC public schools' athletic association.10
    • State level — DCSAA. DC's state athletic association.11

    Virginia

    • Public schools — VHSL. The Virginia High School League runs public-school regions and state championships, with its own basketball calendar.12
    • Private schools — VISAA. The Virginia Independent Schools Athletic Association.13
